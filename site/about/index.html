About

I'm Mark Mathew, a designer and independent researcher.

01 / Practice

How I work

I trained as an industrial designer, and that’s still the foundation of my work. I earned my master’s degree in Industrial and Product Design Management from Rubika in 2019.

I’ve worked across physical products, digital interfaces, immersive experiences, software, and research. I move between observing how people use things, building prototypes, and putting designs into use. What I learn at one stage often sends me back to another.

I’ve worked at Safran Seats since 2019 on passenger and crew experiences. My work there has ranged from industrial and interface design to human–machine interaction, immersive technology, and digital design methods. Much of it involves keeping what people need in view while making decisions about what can be engineered and built.

02 / Inquiry

What I’m researching

Alongside my professional work, I pursue independent research into human–AI interaction, cognition, perception, language, and how people make meaning. I’m drawn to questions that cut across these areas. For example, when does an AI tool help someone understand something, and when does it simply make an explanation sound convincing?

I use experiments to see whether an explanation holds up. I try to be clear about where I’m guessing, and I change my mind when the results point elsewhere. Finding out why something failed can be as useful as getting it to work.

What I’m working toward

I’m interested in how tools, systems, and the conditions around them shape what people can imagine, understand, and create. I want technology to give people more room to act for themselves.

I’m working toward a more independent practice where design, research, and experimentation can inform one another. I want the freedom to choose the methods and medium that fit the question, without being tied to a single discipline or commercial goal.

I’d be glad to hear from you. Get in touch. ↗
