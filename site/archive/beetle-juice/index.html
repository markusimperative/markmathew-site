Beetle Juice.

An identity and can design for an imagined energy drink.

Guiding question
What would an energy drink from a research outpost look like?
Year
2017
Form
Beverage identity and packaging concept
For
An imagined future drinks market

A drink from Dione.

I pictured an energy drink in a refrigerator on a research outpost on Dione, one of Saturn’s moons. Insects were the premise for the imagined drink. The name Beetle Juice gave that unfamiliar idea a word people already knew.

The beetle on the can.

I drew the beetle with bands of colour that follow the curve of its shell. In the packaging studies, the beetle and thin lettering tie together cans in five colours.

Portfolio board, dated 4 June 2017.
