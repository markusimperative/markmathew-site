Breathe.

Exploring urban air quality through a fictional city.

Guiding question
How could public spaces respond to polluted air?
Portfolio
2018
Form
Urban design research
For
People who use public spaces in polluted cities

A city to think with.

I started with a brief to explore what design could do about air quality in public spaces. I drew on research into particulate pollution and used Urbania, a fictional city, to bring housing, traffic, industry, and agriculture into one scenario.

Placing those activities side by side helped me think about where people might encounter pollution in the course of an ordinary day.

Directions to explore.

I looked at existing approaches, from air filtration structures to ways of reusing collected pollution. The chapter ends with four possible directions drawn from biomimicry, reuse, architecture, and social participation.
