A study of how drones could help during a disaster.

Guiding question
How could a drone help someone who’s been cut off?
Year
2017
Form
Disaster response design study
For
People affected by disasters

From delivery to communication.

I co-led this design team with Tanishia Rane in partnership with IdeaForge. The brief began with delivery during a disaster. We studied past responses and mapped when a drone might be useful to someone on the ground.

Seeing it from the ground.

We used role-play to consider what someone might see, hear, or understand when a drone approached. That made communication a design question alongside delivery.

The team brought the scenario studies and research on humanitarian uses of drones together in a manual. The portfolio credits IdeaForge with the project’s intellectual property.
