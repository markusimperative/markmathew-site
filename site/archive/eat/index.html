A Hellmann’s bottle concept for getting to the last squeeze.

Guiding question
How could a bottle help us use what’s left inside?
Portfolio
2018
Form
Packaging concept
For
People using squeeze bottles at home

Where the mayonnaise gets stuck.

I tried a small squeeze bottle and looked at where mayonnaise collected inside it. I sketched alternative forms, nozzles, and ways of moving the contents toward the opening, with the familiar squeeze as a starting point.

A loop and a squeeze.

The selected concept added a loop so the closed bottle could be spun to move the remaining mayonnaise toward the cap. Squeezing would still dispense it. The labels show the proposed sequence and explore a temperature-sensitive colour cue as a reminder to refrigerate the bottle.
