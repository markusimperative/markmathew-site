Golden Moth.

A display typeface drawn from the shapes of a house moth.

Guiding question
How could the shapes of a house moth become an alphabet?
Year
2017
Form
Display typeface
For
Headlines and large lettering

Drawing the moth.

I broke the form of a house moth into simple shapes and rebuilt it as a drawing. I then used the circles, arcs, and lines from that drawing to assemble letters.

A typeface for headlines.

The O is an oval with a small dot at its centre. Letters such as M and W carry small marks inside their open shapes. I designed the face for headlines, where those details have room to show.

Portfolio board, dated 4 June 2017.
