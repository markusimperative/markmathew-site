Iris.

A compact camera concept for people filming themselves.

Guiding question
How could one camera help a creator frame several views?
Portfolio
2018–2019
Form
Vlogging camera concept
For
People making videos on their own

Several views from one place.

I explored a camera for people who record at a desk and need to show themselves as well as what they are doing. The Iris concept put three lenses in a compact round body to offer a wide view, a main view, and a close-up.

Checking the size at a desk.

I moved between CAD and physical form mockups to see how the camera would sit in a workspace. In one mockup, the round body rests along the edge of a monitor.

Working with the edge.

The mount study proposed a grip that could adjust to the thickness of a screen or ledge. That gave the body a place to sit within the setup the creator already used.
