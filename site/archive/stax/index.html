STAX.

A stackable enclosure concept for reused laptop drives.

Guiding question
How could an old laptop drive have a second life?
Portfolio
2018
Form
Modular drive enclosure concept
For
People with old laptop drives

Start with the drive.

The project began with the hard drive inside a retired laptop. I explored how that part could be reused in an enclosure that could sit on a desk and stack with others as more drives became available.

The sketches compare different ways to hold the drive, including a tray and a case made of two parts.

Make the drive’s state visible.

I explored a narrow display on the front of the enclosure so someone could identify a drive in a stack and see its state at a glance. The interface drawings show proposed health, warning, and speed states.

2018 design portfolio, pages 24–32.
