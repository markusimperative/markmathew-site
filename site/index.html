About me

Hello,
I’m Mark.

My work moves between language, software, research, and experimental media, guided by questions that rarely belong to a single discipline.

I develop independent projects to explore those questions, giving ideas form through making and experimentation. Sometimes the result is a tool, sometimes an experience, and sometimes a discovery that changes the question itself.
